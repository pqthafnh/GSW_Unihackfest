# Skill: AI Review

## Workflow
1. Minimize and sanitize the text sent to the provider.
2. Use a provider interface and structured output schema.
3. Request summary, requirement coverage, risk flags and confidence note.
4. Validate provider output.
5. Save review separately from the submission.
6. Treat timeout or invalid output as retryable.
7. Display a human-decision disclaimer.

## Prohibited
No AI-authorship probability, plagiarism certainty, automatic approval, automatic rejection or legal conclusion.
